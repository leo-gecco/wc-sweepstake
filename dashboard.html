Rules

How the sweepstake works

The basics

Ten of us are in at £20 each — a £200 pot. All 48 World Cup teams were drawn live and screen-recorded, so everyone holds five teams. The whole pot is paid out through 20 side bets worth £10 each; there are no entry-fee refunds.

How a bet is held

Each side bet is “held” by whoever owns the team currently leading its metric — most goals, most corners, fastest goal, and so on. Standings update from real full-time match stats (never mid-game) and settle when the tournament ends. The single-game awards can change hands as records fall.

Champion's Bonus 🏆

If two or more punters are tied for a bet, nobody banks it — that £10 rolls into the Champion's Bonus. Any bet that ends with no winner goes there too. The pot is won outright by whoever owns the tournament-winning team, on top of their own bet winnings, and stays unassigned until the final is played.

Co-owned teams

Two teams are shared picks: Japan (Leo & Tom) and Curacao (Jamie & Brownout). Any winnings from a co-owned team are split 50/50 between the two owners. A co-owned team leading a bet on its own still counts as a win and is split — it is not treated as contested.
